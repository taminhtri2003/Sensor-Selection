\section{Sensitivity Analysis: Non-Inferiority Margins}
We performed sensitivity analyses at non-inferiority margins $\delta = 0.01$, $\delta = 0.02$ (primary), and $\delta = 0.03$. The minimum non-inferior channel count $k^*$ remained consistent across margins ($k^*=6$ at $\delta=0.02$).

\section{Quality Control and Dataset Integrity}
All 43 GRABMyo participant recordings and 10 NinaPro DB5 subject files passed zero-NaN and non-flat signal checks. Cryptographic SHA-256 manifests are preserved under \texttt{data/manifests/}.
