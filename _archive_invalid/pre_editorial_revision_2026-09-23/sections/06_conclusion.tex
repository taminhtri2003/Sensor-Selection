\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\chg{For offline subject-independent gesture recognition with the two-ring monopolar GRABMyo montage, reducing the array from 16 to 12 electrodes remained within a prespecified 0.02 Macro-F1 non-inferiority margin, whereas further reduction did not. Cross-fitted attribution identified electrodes whose removal materially affected retrained-model performance and consistently outperformed average random selection, although its advantage over simple spatial selection was limited at most tested subset sizes. Stability regularisation did not provide additional benefit under the present protocol. These findings support moderate, evidence-based electrode reduction rather than aggressive sparsification and highlight the importance of evaluating electrode selection at the participant level under prespecified performance tolerances. Validation in target clinical populations, physical reduced-sensor hardware and online causal-control settings remains necessary before the result can be translated into deployment recommendations.}
