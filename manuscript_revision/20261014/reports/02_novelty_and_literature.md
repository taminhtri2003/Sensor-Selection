# Novelty and literature audit

This is a targeted primary-source check, not a systematic review. Searches combined EMG/sEMG, SHAP/Shapley, channel/electrode selection, participant-independent validation, attribution stability and non-inferiority. The old 93-entry literature inventory is not treated as 93 verified full-text studies. Metadata and protocol verification are recorded separately; an inaccessible method is unknown, not an absent method.

The defensible contribution is an empirically tested participant-disjoint reduction procedure with an explicit mean engineering tolerance, comparisons against random/spatial selection, and transparent failure boundaries. It is not a new SHAP algorithm, a proven beneficial stability penalty, a universally optimal count, or prospective validation of one hardware layout.

New bibliographic JSON records are preserved in reproducibility/literature. The native bibliography uses the print year 2025 for Rani's 2024 online DOI. Primary-source access limitations remain visible; unknown detailed validation protocols are not filled from secondary summaries. Manuscript citations make only the bounded claims supported above.

## Cited-record metadata follow-up

The initial automated audit retrieved16 of21 cited records directly through Crossref. The GRABMyo dataset record was then verified through DataCite (Crossref was not the correct registry), and the DB5 article was verified through its primary PLOS page and a successful Crossref retry. Bengio/Grandvalet and RoSHAP have verified primary journal/arXiv URLs; the conference SHAP record uses its primary proceedings URL. These records and access statuses are retained separately from full-method verification. Metadata identity does not imply complete validation-protocol access.

The closest Rani author abstract was also retrieved from [the primary Padua repository](https://www.research.unipd.it/handle/11577/3545125?mode=complete). Its smaller, seven-channel acquisition and four/six movement tasks are confirmed there; no claim about absent participant-independent validation is drawn from an abstract.
