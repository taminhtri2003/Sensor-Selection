\paragraph{Participant-level attribution extension (post hoc)} \chg{All 43 outer participants were evaluated under both aggregations. The median relative dispersion of attribution ($U_c/A_c$ over all outer-fold and electrode cells) increased from 2.40\% under fold aggregation to 4.62\% under participant aggregation; these cells are not independent replicates, so this change is descriptive. Participant and fold aggregation were compared at every penalty and reduced subset size (60 comparisons); in 42 of them at least one participant's score differed between the two aggregations. After Holm correction across these comparisons, none favoured participant aggregation and none favoured fold aggregation. Results for every penalty are given in supplementary table~S7; no penalty was selected using outer-test performance. These comparisons test the change of aggregation and do not replace the original H3 result. Within participant aggregation, each positive penalty was also compared with attribution magnitude alone (12 Holm-corrected comparisons): none favoured the penalty and none favoured magnitude alone.}
